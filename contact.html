Frequently Answered

Acoustic Clarity & Support

Common questions regarding home trials, 3-year warranty, and compatibility.

What is the 30-Day Risk-Free Home Acoustic Trial?
We encourage you to audition Lumina headphones and audio systems in your personal listening environment. If you are not completely spellbound by the acoustic transparency within 30 days of delivery, we provide a prepaid return courier label and complete 100% refund.
How does the 3-Year Lumina Care Warranty function?
Every authentic Lumina product comes standard with a 3-year international warranty covering all mechanical tolerances, drivers, wireless DSP chips, and battery degradation below 80%. In the unlikely event of a defect, we replace your unit with overnight express courier dispatch.
Are Lumina audio devices compatible with Apple, Windows, and Android?
Yes. All Lumina transducers support universal Bluetooth 5.4 with backward compatibility, LDAC, aptX Lossless, and AAC codecs. The Lumina Companion App is available on iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows for multi-band parametric EQ tuning and firmware updates.
Can I order replacement ear cushions or modular batteries?
Absolutely. Our circular design guarantee means ear cushions, audio cables, and battery packs are modular. You can purchase official replacement parts anytime from our Accessories catalog or request them through your Account Dashboard.
