Origin & Purpose

Where Acoustic Physics Meets
Architectural Elegance

Founded in Zurich in 2021 by a consortium of acoustic engineers and industrial minimalists, Lumina was born from an uncompromising question: Why must professional reference sound be trapped in bulky, disposable plastics?

Guiding Principles

The Lumina Pillars

Every curve, diaphragm, and code release adheres to these four non-negotiables.

Uncolored Acoustic Truth

We do not falsely inflate the bass. We reproduce the artist's recording exactly as heard on the mixing desk console.

Enduring Materials

Aerospace titanium, CNC milled aluminum, and recycled magnesium alloy ensure our pieces outlive planned obsolescence.

Circular Lifecycle

100% modular parts with easily swappable battery units and memory foam cushions so gear is maintained, not discarded.

Zero Data Monopolies
